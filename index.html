SwiftLogistics Terms & Conditions

Standard Freight & Consignment Transit Policies

1. Scope of Courier Services & Service Levels

SwiftLogistics provides intra-city express dispatch from Lipa Central Hub #04 and regional line-haul distribution across Batangas and Region IV-A. Local intra-city deliveries booked before 2:00 PM qualify for Same-Day Express dispatch; bookings made after 2:00 PM are guaranteed for Next-Day morning transit.

2. Cash on Delivery (COD) Remittances

For COD consignments, the recipient must tender exact currency upon parcel handover. Collected funds are cleared and digitally remitted to merchant accounts within twenty-four (24) banking hours. A physical or digital receipt with unique POD token verification is required upon handover.

3. Fragile & Breakable Item Special Handling

Consignments containing glassware, ceramics, liquid containers, delicate electronics, or precision instruments must be declared as Fragile. A non-refundable ₱75.00 Special Handling Surcharge applies. Senders must ensure dual-walled corrugated outer boxing and shock-absorbent cushioning.

4. Weight & Cargo Capacity Limits

Standard motorcycle courier dispatch enforces a strict maximum weight limit of 25.0 kg and 10 items. Shipments between 25.1 kg and 50.0 kg (or up to 15 items) are automatically upgraded to Cargo Van / Light Truck dispatch. Any single consignment exceeding 50.0 kg must be split into multiple separate waybills.

5. Delivery Attempt Limits & Return to Hub

SwiftLogistics couriers execute a maximum of three (3) legitimate delivery attempts. If delivery fails on all three attempts due to customer unavailability, incorrect destination, or gate refusal, the consignment is returned to Lipa Central Hub for sender reclaim.

6. Claims & Loss Liability

Claims for damaged or missing contents must be filed within forty-eight (48) hours of delivery completion with photographic evidence and the active Delivery Verification Token.
